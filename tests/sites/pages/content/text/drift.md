The only page whose `linkTitle` differs from its `title`, and it differs in the
first letter. The `title` and `linktitle` fields are documented as two different
things, so this is the page that shows whether they really are: it falls under
`D` where the title was read and under `S` where the link title was - and every
listing labels it Signal, whichever of the two ordered or grouped it.
