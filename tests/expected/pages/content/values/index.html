Values

What an expression makes of a value that is not plain text: a missing weight, zero, padding, a list, a map, and one key holding numbers and text alike. Five children, Copper, Iron, Lead, Tin and Zinc.

Tin weighs 10, Copper 20 and Iron 30; Lead and Zinc are unweighted. Their rank is the number -1 for Tin, 1 for Lead and 2 for Copper, but the text x for Iron and the text 10 for Zinc.

Every grouped listing here sets headinglevel="4".

weight - a weight of 0 has no value

orderby=weight

Lead and Zinc are unweighted and come last, in Hugo’s order.

Tin and Copper. The unweighted pages are not kept, as a missing value only matches !=.

Numbers compare as numbers, so every weighted page is kept - as text, 10, 20 and 30 would all sort before 9.

Numbers and text in one key

Numbers and text have no common order: Hugo compares text with a number as if it were one, or else as 0, which puts x below 1 and 10 above 2, yet 10 below x. So the numbers are ordered as numbers, then the texts as text, the numbers first in either direction. Zinc’s 10 is text and orders as such.

orderby=params.rank

Tin, Lead and Copper by number, then Zinc and Iron by text.

  • Tin
  • Lead
  • Copper
  • Zinc
  • Iron

orderby=params.rank desc

Copper, Lead and Tin, then Iron and Zinc: both parts reversed, the numbers still first.

  • Copper
  • Lead
  • Tin
  • Iron
  • Zinc

The groups follow the ascending order above.
