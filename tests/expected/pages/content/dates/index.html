              Dates

              Expressions over dates. Nine children, Alpha through India.

              The four date fields are set decades apart on purpose - date in 2020-2023, lastmod in 2024-2027, publishDate in 2018-2021, expiryDate in 2090-2093 - so no two fields can produce the same set of groups. India has none of them.

              Within the five pages that share a date year, lastmod ranks them in the exact reverse of their date order. Ordering inside a group is therefore the one place where naming the wrong field cannot pass for the right one.

              Every listing here sets headinglevel="4".
