                  Layout

                  The tree a listing is taken from, and what a display makes of it. Three children - One, a leaf; Two, a branch with two pages under it; and Three, a branch with one - so a second level exists to be shown or withheld. A fourth, Four, is a hidden branch with one page under it, and absent from every listing that does not ask for hidden pages.
